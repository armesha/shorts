## Canon

The hidden city has magical paw crystals that unlock secret animal routes under parks, old gates,
tram tunnels, clock towers, mirror rivers, clouds, libraries, and giant gardens.

Core team:

- Iskra: orange fox heroine, teal cape, golden paw medallion. Fast, brave, impulsive, funny.
- Badger: sturdy protector in dark gear. Strong, practical, skeptical.
- Owl: investigator with brass goggles. Reads symbols, maps, clocks, and mechanisms.
- Turtle: kind green engineer. Calm, clever with shields and bubbles.
- Bunny: fast scout with expressive reactions.
- Squirrel: tiny energetic helper with comic timing.
- Tiny time kitten: mysterious helper with a silver bell.

Recurring mystery:

- Mirror Iskra: cold duplicate with an upside-down paw medallion. She follows the team after the
  mirror river arc and is still active after episode 11.
- Mechanical magpie: stole or carried a crystal shard around the cloud island/glowing tree.
- Huge cloud shadow: an unrevealed large creature heard the crystal song in episode 09.

## Active Objects

- Paw crystals and shards: glow, sing, project maps, and open routes.
- Iskra's medallion: reacts to crystals and distinguishes the real team from mirror doubles.
- Feather compass: points by comic visual symbols, not normal directions.
- Blank cloud-library book: projected the route to the underground city heart.

## Active Locations

- Old park and abandoned gate: origin of the crystal route.
- Clock tower: created the beacon and mirror-river incident.
- Mirror river: produced the false Iskra.
- Cloud ship and floating island: route to the feather compass and mechanical magpie.
- Moon cloud library: revealed the route below.
- Giant garden: current location; roots opened a path to an icy market.

## Episode Log

1. Signal in the Park: the team forms, finds the first paw crystal, and sees a second crystal stolen.
2. Stone Lions: old stone lions and hidden route symbols pull the team deeper into the city mystery.
3. Underground Tram: the hidden route becomes an underground moving system under the city.
4. Rooftop Chase: the chase rises above the city and points toward the clock tower.
5. Beacon Tower: the tower turns into a golden beacon; the river becomes a mirror; a false Iskra appears.
6. River Double: mirror doubles mimic the team; laughter weakens them, but false Iskra follows unseen.
7. Time Kitten: time freezes; a kitten with a silver bell opens the impossible attic route.
8. Cloud Ship: the team reaches a ship in the clouds; a mechanical magpie holds a stolen shard.
9. Feather Compass: the glowing tree becomes stairs; a feather compass points to the cloud library;
   a huge shadow hears the crystal song.
10. Moon Library: flying books and a blank book reveal the route below the library into a giant garden.
11. Giant Garden: the team lands in giant grass, meets a forgetful snail guard, and finds a root path
   to an icy market while mirror Iskra follows them.
