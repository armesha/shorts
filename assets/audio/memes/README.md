# Meme and humor audio beds

This folder contains short background beds for meme, joke and funny-quote packs.

## CC0 source beds

- `bed-001.mp3` through `bed-100.mp3` are short cuts from CC0/public-domain-dedicated recordings on archive.org.
- Per-file provenance is stored in `_beds-cc0-provenance.json`.
- The ledger records source URL, download URL, original file name, composer, performer/author, license, cut point and duration.
- Keep `_beds-cc0-provenance.json` with these files when moving or replacing them.

## Local synth beds

The remaining short files, such as `pad_*`, `lofi_*`, `arp_*`, `box_*`, `pluck_playful.mp3` and `quirky_*`, are local project synth beds for lightweight variation. They should stay instrumental, non-branded and free of vocals/samples.

## Rules

- Do not add copyrighted songs, famous meme audio, TV/movie/game audio, or unclear-rights samples.
- New downloaded tracks need a source/license record beside the file.
- New generated/synth beds should be documented here or in a sibling provenance file.
- Keep volume low enough that TTS or readable card pacing remains clear.
